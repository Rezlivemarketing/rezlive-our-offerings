03 / Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Who can use the RezLive.com hotel platform?

RezLive.com is a B2B reservation platform intended for travel agents and other eligible travel trade partners.

Does RezLive.com offer more than traditional hotels?

Its published accommodation offering includes hotels, apartments and several property categories. Options depend on the destination and available inventory.

What are Smart Match and RezTrust?

Smart Match supports hotel room comparisons, while RezTrust provides dynamic reliability information for booking options. Both are explained on dedicated RezLive.com pages.
