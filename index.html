05 / Good to know

Questions, answered.

A few essentials about working with RezLive.com and exploring our services.

What is RezLive.com?

RezLive.com is a B2B online reservation platform for eligible travel agents and travel partners. Its offering includes accommodation, sightseeing, ground services, rail, cruises and other travel products.

Who can register for RezLive.com?

RezLive.com is intended for professionals in the tourism sector. Agencies can begin with the official agent registration process and follow the applicable eligibility requirements.

Can I plan different services for the same client?

The platform includes several travel product categories, allowing agencies to plan accommodation, activities and transport components. Product availability and booking conditions vary.

Which packages are featured on this page?

The Packages section focuses on Europamundo tours offered through RezLive.com. Available tours, departure dates and inclusions should be confirmed through the agent channel.

How can my business connect to hotel content?

RezTez provides XML/JSON hotel content integration for eligible travel businesses. Visit the official XML Connect page for integration enquiries.
